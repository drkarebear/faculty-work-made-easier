About the project

Independent faculty projects, built from real problems.

Most of these projects begin because I am trying to do something as a faculty member and realize the process is more confusing, repetitive, or time-consuming than it needs to be.

Faculty Work Made Easier is the home for those projects: practical tools designed to reduce the hidden labor around teaching, OER, data, accessibility, and navigating college systems.

KC
Karen Crozer California Community College English faculty member and independent project maintainer
Useful before impressive The goal is not to make faculty work look more complicated. It is to make the next step clearer.
Faculty judgment stays central AI may support first-pass searching or development, but consequential decisions remain human.
Accessibility is part of the design Semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive reflow, and descriptive links are built in.
