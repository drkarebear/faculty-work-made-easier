About this site

Privacy

Faculty Work Made Easier is a public, independently maintained faculty resource. You do not need an account to browse this site.

What this site collects

This hub does not run a first-party account system, mailing list, advertising tracker, form, or site-analytics service. You can browse the core site without submitting personal information to me.

GitHub Pages

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like other web-hosting services, GitHub may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, such as IP address, browser or device information, and access logs. That information is handled by GitHub under its own policies rather than by a Faculty Work Made Easier analytics system.

External project links

The homepage links to separate websites and resources, including Data Mart Smart, CCC English OER Picks, The OER Promptbook, and course-resource sites. Those destinations may have their own privacy statements, third-party services, or data practices. Their privacy terms apply when you visit or use them.

External AI tools and services

Some linked resources discuss or link to AI tools, library services, forms, or other external websites. I do not control how those services collect, store, or use information.

Privacy reminder: Avoid entering confidential student, employee, or institutional information into public AI tools, forms, or other external services. Review your college or district policies and the privacy settings of the services you use.

Cookies and advertising

Faculty Work Made Easier does not intentionally place first-party advertising cookies and does not sell personal information. Hosting providers or external destinations may operate under their own technical and privacy practices.

Questions and maintenance

Faculty Work Made Easier is independently created and maintained by Karen Crozer. This privacy statement may be updated if the site adds new features or services.
